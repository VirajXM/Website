Category: Whiskey

ROULETTE Premium Whisky is a premium blended Indian whisky crafted in Goa using carefully selected grain spirit and quality malt whiskies. Matured in imported American ex bourbon oak casks, it delivers exceptional smoothness, depth, and character. Available in both Peated and Unpeated expressions, the range offers either rich smoky complexity or a refined, fruit forward profile, making it an ideal choice for premium retail, hospitality, and international markets.

Sizes available: 60ml, 180ml, 200ml, 750ml

Category: Whiskey

Dennis Special Whisky is a smooth blended whisky crafted for exceptional balance and everyday enjoyment. Bottled at 42.8% ABV, it presents a vibrant golden amber colour with a fresh aroma and subtle hints of peat. The palate is smooth and well rounded, offering gentle warmth and a clean, lingering finish. Whether enjoyed neat, on the rocks, or with water or soda, Dennis Special Whisky delivers a refined and approachable whisky experience.

Sizes available: 750ml

Category: Wine

Goana's Wine is a premium fortified wine crafted to deliver a rich, smooth, and satisfying drinking experience. With an ABV of 14%, it showcases a deep ruby red colour, complemented by inviting aromas of ripe berries and subtle spice. The palate is medium sweet with balanced acidity and a long, pleasant finish, making it an excellent pairing for cheeses, grilled meats, desserts, spicy cuisine, and light snacks. Best served chilled between 12–16°C for optimal enjoyment.

Sizes available: 750ml

Category: Whiskey

Mont Castle Whisky is a premium blended Indian whisky crafted for a smooth, well balanced drinking experience. Bottled at 42.8% ABV, it features a bright golden amber colour with inviting aromas of light oak, vanilla, and caramel. The palate is mellow and slightly sweet, with subtle notes of malt and oak leading to a warm, lingering finish. Ideal for enjoying neat, on the rocks, or with water or soda, Mont Castle Whisky is a versatile choice for retail, hospitality, and international export markets.

Sizes available: 180ml, 375ml, 750ml

Category: Whiskey

AC Black & Aristocrat Premium Whiskies are expertly crafted Indian Made Foreign Liquors (IMFL), blended with imported Scotch malts and select Indian grain spirits to deliver exceptional smoothness and character. Bottled at 42.8% ABV, both whiskies feature rich golden amber hues with inviting notes of malt, vanilla, caramel, and gentle oak, leading to a smooth, balanced finish. Available in two premium expressions – AC Black Pure Grain Whisky and Aristocrat Premium Whisky – the range offers a refined whisky experience, perfect for enjoying neat, on the rocks, or with water or soda.

Sizes available: 180ml, 375ml, 750ml, 1L

Category: Vodka

IceX Vodka is a premium vodka crafted from 100% pure grain spirit and triple distilled to achieve exceptional smoothness and purity. Using advanced multi stage filtration with carbon, silver, and platinum filtration technology, IceX Vodka delivers a clean, refined finish with a balanced character. Available at 42.8% ABV (subject to export market regulations), the range includes Original, Green Apple, Orange, Chilli, and Bare It All Premium Variant, offering a versatile selection designed for modern vodka consumers and international markets.

Sizes available: 180ml, 375ml, 750ml
